Security Analysis

STRIDE Threat Model

A formal security analysis mapping each STRIDE threat category to our four-pillar defense, demonstrating comprehensive coverage.

S

Spoofing

Identity Impersonation

⚡ Attack Vector

Attacker impersonates a patient or healthcare worker to access medical records, file fake insurance claims, or prescribe unauthorized treatments.

🛡️ Our Defense

Aadhaar Biometric Auth On-Chain Identity Verification

Multi-factor Aadhaar biometric authentication (fingerprint + iris) at the point of care. Every identity claim is cross-verified against the blockchain-stored credential hash, making impersonation physically impossible.

Residual Risk:
Very Low (8%)
T

Tampering

Data Modification

⚡ Attack Vector

Attackers modify patient records in transit or at rest — altering diagnoses, medication dosages, lab results, or insurance claim amounts to commit fraud.

🛡️ Our Defense

QKD Encrypted Channel Immutable Blockchain Ledger

Data-in-transit is protected by QKD — any interception attempt destroys the quantum state, alerting both parties. Data-at-rest is hashed and stored on an append-only blockchain. Altering even one byte invalidates the entire chain.

Residual Risk:
Negligible (3%)
R

Repudiation

Denied Actions

⚡ Attack Vector

A doctor or patient denies performing an action — e.g., "I never accessed that record" or "I never authorized that prescription" — to evade accountability.

🛡️ Our Defense

Timestamped Blockchain Audit Trail Biometric Non-Repudiation

Every transaction is logged on the blockchain with an immutable timestamp and biometric signature. The combination of Aadhaar identity + blockchain record creates an undeniable, court-admissible audit trail.

Residual Risk:
Very Low (5%)
I

Information Disclosure

Data Leakage

⚡ Attack Vector

"Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" — adversaries intercept and store encrypted health data today, planning to decrypt it once quantum computers become available.

🛡️ Our Defense

Physics-Based QKD Encryption Post-Quantum Cryptography (Kyber)

QKD keys are derived from quantum physics — they cannot be retroactively broken by any future computer. PQC (Kyber-512) protects data-at-rest with lattice-based math that is resistant to both classical and quantum attacks.

Residual Risk:
Negligible (2%)
D

Denial of Service

System Disruption

⚡ Attack Vector

DDoS attacks flood the health network, making patient records inaccessible during emergencies. Ransomware locks hospital systems, demanding cryptocurrency payments.

🛡️ Our Defense

AI Anomaly Detection (Isolation Forest) Decentralized Architecture

AI monitoring detects DDoS patterns and traffic anomalies in real-time, triggering automatic mitigation. The decentralized blockchain architecture ensures no single point of failure — even if one node goes down, the ledger persists across all others.

Residual Risk:
Low (15%)
E

Elevation of Privilege

Unauthorized Access

⚡ Attack Vector

A low-level user (e.g., receptionist) exploits a vulnerability to gain doctor-level or admin-level access, enabling them to view/modify sensitive records or system configurations.

🛡️ Our Defense

Role-Based Aadhaar Credentials AI Behavioral Analysis Smart Contract Access Control

Access is governed by Aadhaar-linked role credentials enforced by blockchain smart contracts. AI continuously monitors user behavior patterns — a receptionist querying oncology records triggers immediate investigation. Privilege changes require multi-signature blockchain consensus.

Residual Risk:
Low (10%)
